Published work

2024
Published book chapter · CRC Press / Taylor & Francis

The Evaluation and Suggestions for the Curative Methods of Space Design for Early Schizophrenia Patients with the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) Approach

This chapter investigates architectural considerations in therapeutic environments for people with early schizophrenia, including natural light, privacy, greenery and colour. It reflects an early interest in formalising spatial design decisions through a research framework.

Selected academic essays

2024
Unpublished coursework

The Evaluation and Suggestion of Inclusive Public Space Design for Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD) Group

Case study: Green City Square, Zhengzhou. The essay explores how greenery, water, natural sound and spatial management might support a more accommodating public environment for people experiencing social anxiety.

Unpublished coursework

The Architectural Design Approach Acts as Milieu Therapy to Optimise Emotional Adjustment for People with Depression

Comparative discussion of Kronstad Psychiatric Hospital and the University of Liverpool’s Electrical Engineering and Electronics Building, focusing on sunlight, transparency and colour as spatial strategies.
